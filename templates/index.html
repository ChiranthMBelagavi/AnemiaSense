About Anemia and Predictive Metrics

Anemia is a condition where you lack healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin, reducing oxygen supply in the body. Symptoms include fatigue, weakness, shortness of breath, and pale skin. Causes range from nutritional deficiencies to chronic diseases. Treatments vary depending on the type and severity.

Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin transports oxygen in red blood cells. Normal: Men 13.2-16.6 g/dL, Women 11.6-15 g/dL.

MCH

Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin: average hemoglobin per red cell. Normal: 27.5–33.2 pg.

MCHC

Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration: hemoglobin concentration in red cells. Normal: 33.4–35.5 g/dL.

MCV

Mean Corpuscular Volume: average size of red blood cells. Normal: 80–100 fL.
